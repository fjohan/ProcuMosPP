Whisper + ProcuMosPP

Text tells you what was said. Prominence tells you how it was said.

This page presents ProcuMosPP as a complementary layer to Whisper. Whisper recovers words and timing; ProcuMosPP adds word-level prominence from the audio signal.

Three framing questions

What kind of technology is it?

A speech ML inference pipeline for prosodic prominence prediction. It combines acoustic features and transformer-based speech representations to estimate emphasis per word.

What can it be used for?

Enriching transcripts, speech analytics, prosody-aware annotation, and downstream systems where emphasis matters (for example, expressive TTS workflows).

What questions can it answer?

Which words were emphasized? Where are prominence peaks in time? How does emphasis differ between readings of the same content?

How the modeling process works

Workflow animation: human labeling -> aggregation -> training -> prediction.

  1. Human labeling: users assign labels to raw data.
  2. Aggregation: individual labels are combined into robust statistics.
  3. Training: a model learns from aggregated human labels.
  4. Prediction: model output is compared with human labels.

In deployment, this becomes: Audio -> Whisper (text + timestamps) -> ProcuMosPP (prominence) -> enriched transcript.
